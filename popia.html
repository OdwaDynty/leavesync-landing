How LeaveSync complies with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013

Last updated: 4 June 2026 | Act reference: POPIA — Act 4 of 2013

✅ LeaveSync is fully compliant with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa. This page explains exactly how we comply and what this means for you and your employees.

What is POPIA?

The Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) is South Africa's data protection law, enacted on 1 July 2021. It governs how organisations collect, store, use and share personal information about South African residents.

POPIA gives individuals (data subjects) rights over their personal information and places obligations on organisations (responsible parties) that process that information.

Lawful Basis for Processing

We process personal information on the following lawful grounds under POPIA Section 11:

  • Contract: Processing is necessary to fulfil our service agreement with your company
  • Legitimate interest: Security logging and fraud prevention
  • Legal obligation: Audit trail retention as required by law

Your Employees' Rights

Under POPIA every employee whose data is stored in LeaveSync has the right to:

  • Know that their information is being processed (your responsibility as employer to inform them)
  • Access their personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of their data (subject to retention requirements)
  • Object to processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator

Data Breach Response

In the event of a data breach that is likely to affect data subjects we will:

  • Notify the Information Regulator within 72 hours of becoming aware
  • Notify affected data subjects as soon as reasonably possible
  • Take immediate steps to contain the breach
  • Conduct a root cause analysis and implement corrective measures

Your Obligations as a Company Using LeaveSync

When you use LeaveSync to process your employees' personal information you become a responsible party under POPIA. This means:

  • You must inform employees that their data is stored in a cloud-based system
  • You must have a lawful reason to process employee data (employment contract is sufficient)
  • You must respond to employee POPIA requests within a reasonable time
  • You must keep access to the system limited to authorised personnel only
