Data Processing Agreement

POPIA-compliant operator agreement between LeaveSync and its customers

Version 1.0 · Effective: 1 June 2026 · Governed by POPIA Act 4 of 2013

This Data Processing Agreement (DPA) forms part of the Terms of Service between DigitSkillSolutions Pty Ltd (operator of LeaveSync) and the subscribing organisation (responsible party). It is required by POPIA Section 20 and 21.

1. Definitions

  • Responsible Party: The subscribing organisation that determines the purpose and means of processing personal information (your company)
  • Operator: DigitSkillSolutions Pty Ltd, trading as LeaveSync, which processes personal information on behalf of the responsible party
  • Personal Information: Employee names, email addresses, leave records, job titles, department information and other data processed through LeaveSync
  • Processing: Any operation performed on personal information including collection, storage, retrieval, use, modification and deletion

2. Roles and Responsibilities

2.1 Responsible Party (Your Company)

You are the responsible party under POPIA. This means you:

  • Determine why and how employee personal information is processed
  • Are responsible for informing employees that their data is stored in LeaveSync
  • Are responsible for ensuring you have a lawful basis to process employee data (employment contract is sufficient)
  • Are responsible for responding to employee POPIA requests within 30 days

2.2 Operator (LeaveSync)

DigitSkillSolutions Pty Ltd is the operator. This means we:

  • Process personal information only on your documented instruction
  • Do not process your data for any purpose other than providing the LeaveSync service
  • Implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures
  • Do not disclose your data to third parties without your authorisation
  • Notify you of a data breach within 72 hours of becoming aware of it
  • Delete or return all personal information upon termination of the service

3. Personal Information Processed

LeaveSync processes the following categories of personal information on your behalf:

  • Employee first and last names
  • Work email addresses
  • Job titles and department information
  • Phone numbers (optional)
  • Leave records including dates, types, reasons and approval history
  • Medical certificate documents uploaded by employees (stored in private file storage)
  • System usage logs including IP addresses and action timestamps

LeaveSync does not process: ID numbers, salary information, bank details, or any financial data.

4. Purpose of Processing

Personal information is processed solely for the purpose of providing leave management services as described in the LeaveSync Terms of Service. No personal information is processed for any other purpose including marketing, research or sale to third parties.

5. Security Measures

DigitSkillSolutions Pty Ltd implements the following technical and organisational security measures:

5.1 Technical Measures

  • All passwords hashed using bcrypt with 12 salt rounds — no plaintext passwords are ever stored
  • All data transmitted over HTTPS with TLS encryption
  • JWT authentication tokens with 7-day expiry
  • Multi-tenant data isolation — each company's data is logically separated using company_id filtering on all database queries
  • Rate limiting to prevent brute force attacks
  • HTTP security headers via Helmet.js
  • Immutable audit log of all sensitive actions
  • Private file storage with signed URL access (documents expire after 1 hour)

5.2 Organisational Measures

  • Role-based access control limiting data access to authorised personnel only
  • Database access restricted to production systems only
  • All third-party sub-processors bound by their own security certifications
  • Designated Information Officer responsible for data protection compliance

6. Sub-Processors

LeaveSync uses the following sub-processors to deliver the service. Each is bound by their own security certifications and policies:

  • Supabase Inc. (USA): Database and file storage. SOC 2 Type II certified. Data hosted in eu-west-2 (London).
  • Render Inc. (USA): API server hosting. SOC 2 Type II certified.
  • Vercel Inc. (USA): Web application hosting. ISO 27001 certified.
  • Google LLC (USA): Email delivery via Gmail SMTP. ISO 27001 certified.
  • PayFast (Pty) Ltd (SA): Payment processing. PCI-DSS compliant. South Africa based.

You authorise the use of these sub-processors by accepting the Terms of Service. We will notify you of any changes to sub-processors with 30 days notice.

7. Data Transfers

Personal information is stored primarily in the United Kingdom (London) on Supabase infrastructure. The UK maintains adequate data protection standards recognised internationally.

Enterprise customers requiring data residency in South Africa may request a dedicated deployment arrangement. Contact us at privacy@leavesync.co.za

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Active accounts: data retained for the duration of the subscription
  • Cancelled accounts: data retained for 90 days then permanently deleted
  • Audit logs: retained for 5 years as required by POPIA Section 14
  • Data export: available at any time on request within 5 business days

9. Data Subject Rights

When your employees exercise their POPIA rights (access, correction, deletion) you are the responsible party and must respond. LeaveSync will assist you by:

  • Providing data exports for access requests within 5 business days of your instruction
  • Deleting specific employee records within 5 business days of your verified instruction
  • Correcting inaccurate data within 2 business days of your instruction

10. Breach Notification

In the event of a personal information breach:

  • We will notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach
  • We will provide details of what data was affected, how it occurred, and what steps we are taking
  • You are responsible for notifying the Information Regulator and affected employees where required by POPIA
  • We will cooperate fully with any investigation

11. Term and Termination

This DPA remains in effect for the duration of your LeaveSync subscription. Upon termination we will provide a complete data export within 5 business days and permanently delete all your data within 90 days.
