🔒 Security at LeaveSync

How we protect your company and employee data

We understand that you are trusting us with sensitive employee information. Here is exactly how we protect it.

🔐
Encryption at Rest and in Transit
All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt — we cannot read them even if we wanted to.
🏢
Complete Data Isolation
Your company's data is completely isolated from all other companies. Every query is filtered by your unique company ID. It is technically impossible for another company to see your data.
👁️
Full Audit Trail
Every sensitive action is logged with a timestamp and the user responsible. You can see who accessed what data and when — providing accountability that spreadsheets cannot offer.
🛡️
Role-Based Access Control
Employees see only their own leave. Managers see only their team. HR admins see the whole company. No one can access data beyond their assigned role.
🇿🇦
POPIA Compliance
Built around all 8 POPIA conditions. Information Officer registered with the SA Information Regulator. Full Data Processing Agreement available.
💾
Daily Backups
Your data is backed up daily with point-in-time recovery available. If anything goes wrong we can restore your complete data within hours.
