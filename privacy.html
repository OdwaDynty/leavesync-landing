Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information

Last updated: 4 June 2026 | Effective date: 4 June 2026

This Privacy Policy is governed by the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) of South Africa. By using LeaveSync you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Who We Are

LeaveSync is a product of DigitSkillSolutions Pty Ltd, a company registered in South Africa.

  • Company: DigitSkillSolutions Pty Ltd
  • Information Officer: Odwa Dyantyi
  • Email: privacy@leavesync.co.za
  • Country: South Africa

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Company name and subdomain
  • Administrator name and email address
  • Password (stored as a bcrypt hash — we cannot read your password)

2.2 Employee Information

  • First name, last name, email address
  • Department, job title, phone number
  • Role within the system
  • Leave balances and leave request history

2.3 Usage Data

  • IP addresses (for rate limiting and security)
  • Actions taken in the system (audit log)
  • Login timestamps

2.4 Payment Information

Payment processing is handled by PayFast (Pty) Ltd. We do not store credit card details. We receive only a payment confirmation and your chosen subscription plan.

2.5 Uploaded Documents

Medical certificates and other documents uploaded by employees are stored securely in Supabase Storage and accessible only to authorised users within your company.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the LeaveSync service
  • To send email notifications about leave requests and approvals
  • To process subscription payments
  • To maintain an audit trail for POPIA compliance
  • To improve our service
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Database: Supabase (hosted in EU West — London)
  • Files: Supabase Storage (private bucket, signed URL access)
  • API: Render.com (hosted in US West — Oregon)
  • Frontend: Vercel (global CDN)

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with 12 salt rounds. JWT tokens expire after 7 days. All sensitive actions are logged in an immutable audit trail.

5. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained for the duration of the subscription
  • Cancelled accounts: Data retained for 12 months after cancellation then permanently deleted
  • Audit logs: Retained for 5 years as required by POPIA Section 14
  • Password reset tokens: Automatically deleted after 1 hour or after use

6. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject under POPIA you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of all personal information we hold about you
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Right to object: Object to the processing of your information
  • Right to complain: Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights email us at privacy@leavesync.co.za

7. Third-Party Services

  • PayFast: Payment processing — PayFast Privacy Policy
  • Supabase: Database and file storage — Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google (Gmail): Email delivery — Google Privacy Policy

8. Cookies

LeaveSync does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. We use localStorage to store your authentication token locally on your device. This is cleared when you log out.

9. Children's Privacy

LeaveSync is a business application intended for use by adults in a workplace context. We do not knowingly collect information from persons under 18 years of age.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users by email at least 30 days before significant changes take effect.

11. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or to exercise your POPIA rights contact our Information Officer:

  • Email: privacy@leavesync.co.za
